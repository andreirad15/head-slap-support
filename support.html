Playing the duel

Hold SLAP to charge, then release to choose the power. Watch the other hand and use DODGE at the right moment. A perfect dodge can open a quick counter. Win two rounds to take the match.

In solo play, combo x2 earns Guard, a critical hit at 97% or above earns Focus, a perfect dodge earns Recover, and combo x3 fills Super Palm. Earned trick charges cost no tokens. Each type can be awarded once per round, and one unused charge per type can carry into the next round.

Why did a heavy slap miss?

Dizziness can send a heavy solo attack off target. In normal solo modes it starts after the eye injury; in Dizzy Duel, strong hits can also cause it earlier. Release below 85% power for a steady aim while dizzy. An earned counter and Super Palm keep their own rules. Online duels do not use the solo Dizzy miss rolls or trick boosts.

Online play and waiting for an opponent

Public matchmaking needs another player. If nobody is available, you can cancel and play a bot duel or another solo mode. For a friend match, one player hosts and shares the invitation code; the other joins with that code. Both players confirm READY before the duel begins.

Use a nickname that does not contain your real name or contact details. The nickname is shown to the opponent. There is no chat or voice feature. If you encounter an inappropriate nickname or an online problem, leave the match and email support with the time and a short description. This support route is not a claim that the current app has an automatic moderation or persistent player-blocking system.

Progress and privacy

Solo progress is stored locally. Reinstalling or clearing app data can remove it; the game does not provide general cloud-sync recovery for your local saves. Anonymous online identity and ranking data are separate from the local save.

Open PRIVACY & SUPPORT for the privacy policy, support, a locally copied Player ID and online-account deletion controls. A deletion request pauses online play while it is processed; it does not reset your solo save. An offline or pending status is not a completed deletion. You can also request deletion by email, including on older versions without the controls. Uninstalling alone is not a backend deletion request. Read the Privacy policy for details.

Sound, haptics and performance

Use the pause/settings controls to turn sound or haptics off. If movement is slow or a control behaves unexpectedly, include your device model, app version, game mode and when it happened in your support message. A short recording is optional; check that it contains no private notifications or account information before sending it.

Shop, ads and purchases

Advertising and purchases are disabled in the current build. The shop may show cosmetic previews with unavailable offers. Those previews do not take a payment or unlock an item. If the behavior in your installed version differs, tell support which version and platform you are using.
